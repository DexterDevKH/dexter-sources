HELP & FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
How do I add this source to SideStore?

Simply tap the Add to SideStore button above. SideStore will open automatically and prompt you to import the repository. You can also copy the Source URL manually, open SideStore > Sources > tap + > paste the URL.

Why are there two separate sources?

Both DTLite Plus and DTKACE Plus are modifications of YouTube sharing the bundle ID com.google.ios.youtube. SideStore and AltStore require unique bundle IDs per source manifest. DTKACE Plus is hosted on its own manifest so you can install either tweak mod without repository identifier collisions.

How to fix "Untrusted Developer" error?

On your iOS device, navigate to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. Under Developer App, select your Apple ID or signing certificate, then tap Trust.
