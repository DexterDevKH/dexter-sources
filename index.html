How do I add this source to SideStore?

Simply tap the Add to SideStore button at the top of this page while browsing on your iPhone or iPad. SideStore will launch and prompt you to accept adding this repository. Alternatively, copy the Source JSON URL, open SideStore > Sources > tap + > paste the URL.

How to fix "Untrusted Developer" error?

On your iOS device, navigate to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. Select your Apple ID / developer certificate under Developer App, and tap Trust.
